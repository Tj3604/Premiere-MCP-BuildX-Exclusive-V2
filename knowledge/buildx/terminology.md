# Terminology

Defines the vocabulary BuildX content uses — domain terms, their on-screen forms, and the
transcription correction lexicon.

**Source-of-truth: authoritative.** No document in this repository holds a glossary or a
correction lexicon. Both were reconstructed from the transcripts and from per-clip notes in
the project clip plans.

**This file has two jobs:**

1. **Say it right.** Domain vocabulary, with the form that goes on screen.
2. **Don't repeat a known mistake.** Mis-transcriptions that recur, with their verified
   corrections.

The glossary and correction lexicon should evolve continuously as new projects introduce new
terminology.

The second is the compounding one. The same transcription model, on the same speaker,
discussing the same subject, garbles the same words on every shoot. **A correction recorded
once is a correction never made again.**

## Domain glossary

Two columns matter: what is **said on camera**, and what goes **on screen**. They are often
not the same — spoken language is long-form, on-screen text is compressed.

### Regulation and permitting

| Term | Spoken as | On screen | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ADU** | "accessory dwelling unit" or "ADU" | **ADU** | Always the initialism on screen. Never "A.D.U." |
| **GFA** | "gross floor area" | **GFA** or **GROSS FLOOR AREA** | Spell out on first use in a piece if the whole clip turns on it |
| **Setbacks** | "setbacks" | **SETBACKS** | Required distance from property lines |
| **Wetlands** | "wetlands" | **WETLANDS** | Triggers conservation review |
| **Ledge** | "ledge" | **LEDGE** | Bedrock near the surface — a site-cost driver |
| **Conservation commission** | "conservation" | **CONSERVATION** | Local body governing wetlands and clearing |
| **Planning board** | "planning board" | **PLANNING BOARD** | Adds review time |
| **Bylaw** | "the local bylaw" | **BYLAW** | Town-level rule; distinct from state law |
| **AG's office** | "the AG's office" | **ATTORNEY GENERAL** | Reviews and approves local bylaws in Massachusetts |
| **Building permit** | "building permit" | **PERMIT** | The gate the first payment tranche buys |
| **Clearing limit** | "maximum you can clear" | **CLEARING LIMIT** | Threshold above which tree clearing needs its own permit |

### Ownership and land

| Term | Spoken as | On screen | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Condominium association** | "condominium association", "condo association" | **CONDO ASSOCIATION** | The ownership structure discussed for selling an ADU separately |
| **Exclusive use area** | "exclusive use property area" | **EXCLUSIVE USE** | The condo-structure term that makes separate sale conceivable |
| **Ground lease** | "ground lease" | **GROUND LEASE** | The long-lease alternative to a condo split |
| **Deed** | "on the deed" | **DEED** | Relevant to multigenerational arrangements |

## Transcription lexicon

Known mis-transcriptions, with what was actually said. **Check this before captioning any
line that reads oddly.** If a correction is not present here, follow the escalation path in
`captions.md` rather than creating a new interpretation.

Automatic transcription does not signal uncertainty — it produces a confident, plausible,
wrong word. Every entry below was originally delivered as ordinary-looking text.

### Verified corrections

| Transcript produced | Actually | Context |
|---|---|---|
| "not in the clothes" | **"enclosed"** | Explaining why a covered porch is excluded from gross floor area — *"not heated and not enclosed"* |
| "loom and seed" | **"loam and seed"** | Landscaping included in the all-in price |
| "to the media down the road" | **"to the ADU"** | Running a gas line from the main house |

### Unresolved — do not caption without checking

**None of these may be guessed.** Resolve by listening, or cut the phrase.

| Transcript produced | Status | Context |
|---|---|---|
| "in a situation" | Likely a **town name** — plausibly Scituate, an actual South Shore town. **Unconfirmed.** | Planning-board review adding time |
| "Pumet" | **Unknown town.** No confident reading. | The location of the first build |
| "the bubble of a bomb" | **Unresolved.** No plausible reading. | Mid-list, describing what the price includes |
| "sell to use or two or three" | **Unresolved.** Garbled mid-sentence. | Advice on asking a builder for client references |
| "you can conduit in the future" | **Unresolved.** Possibly *"condo it"* given the surrounding condominium discussion, but **this is a hypothesis, not a finding.** | The claim that an ADU could be sold separately |
| "[LS]" | **Unresolved.** A dropped token, not a word. | Listing what the pre-permit spend covers |

> The "conduit" line sits inside one of the strongest pieces of content in the library. If it
> resolves to "condo it," the sentence becomes considerably clearer. **It has not been
> verified and must not be captioned on the strength of the hypothesis alone.**

### Fact corrections, not transcription errors

Distinct category: cases where the transcript is *accurate* but the spoken figure was
corrected afterwards.

| Transcript | Corrected to | Note |
|---|---|---|
| "950 sq ft" | **900 sq ft** | Corrected directly. **On a business fact, defer to the correction, not the transcript.** |

Figures and their required qualifiers live in `verified-facts.md`. **Check there before any
number is burned in.**

## Naming rules

Two hard rules, both with a history of near-misses.

### Never name a place that was not named

If a town, street or location was not said clearly on camera, **it does not go on screen.**
This applies even when the answer seems obvious from context.

Two live cases sit in the lexicon above. Both must be resolved or cut — never inferred.

Separately: where a statistic concerns a specific town but the town is not named aloud,
**the statistic ships without the town.** Naming it adds nothing and asserts something the
speaker deliberately did not.

### Never name a person who was not identified

Covered in full by `people.md`. Restated here because the failure mode is identical: an
automatic transcript produces plausible proper nouns, and a plausible proper noun in a
caption reads as fact.

## Maintaining this file

**Add every newly resolved correction to the lexicon.** This is a step in the caption
workflow, not an optional tidy-up — see `captions.md`.

For each entry record: what the transcript produced, what was actually said, and enough
context to recognise the line again. If a correction was reached by listening rather than
by reference, say so — the next editor should know whether they are trusting a document or
a recording.

**Move an entry out of "unresolved" only when it has been confirmed**, never when it has been
guessed convincingly. If there is any uncertainty, leave the entry unresolved and document
the uncertainty rather than the assumption.
