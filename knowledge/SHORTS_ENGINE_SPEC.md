# Shorts Engine Specification

## Candidate types
- Native: one continuous self-contained section.
- Constructed: an exact-quote hook combined with a later setup, proof, or payoff.
- Question-led: concise question followed by the strongest complete answer.
- Myth/reframe: misconception followed by correction.
- Story: conflict, emotional turn, and resolution.
- Authority: specific process, cost, legal, design, or construction insight.

## Quantity strategy
Generate up to 40 high-confidence candidates plus a lower-confidence reserve list. Do not force a fixed number when the source does not support it. Preserve alternate hooks when they produce meaningfully different openings.

## Hook standards
The first 1-3 seconds should deliver at least one of: contradiction, high-stakes consequence, specific promise, surprising fact, emotional tension, or a clear audience problem.

## Intercut restrictions
- Exact transcript quotes only.
- Never fabricate connective dialogue.
- Never reverse cause and effect.
- Never move a reaction away from its authentic moment.
- Allow removal of filler and repeated clauses when meaning is unchanged.
- Maintain a source manifest for every edit beat.
